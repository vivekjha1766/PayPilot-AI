## Lower-Third Caption Design System
- **Positioning**: Center-bottom anchored (width: 1,180px, height: 74px, margin-bottom: 40px)
- **Palette**: Deep Navy (`#102A43` with 92% opacity), Brand Blue accent border (`#0070E0`)
- **Typography**:
  - Category Header: 12pt Bold Uppercase in Electric Cyan (`#00C8FF`)
  - Main Description: 17pt SemiBold in Clean White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Padding**: 20px horizontal, 14px vertical with 4px blue accent pillar on left edge
